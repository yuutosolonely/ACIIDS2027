## Publisher AI policy — applicability and limits

ACIIDS's public submission page does not set out a separate AI-use rule. Springer Nature's book policy applies a risk-based framework. It permits assistive use and permits evaluative or interpretive use with human oversight and transparency; it does not permit opaque use or delegating accountable scholarly judgment. AI systems cannot be authors. Authors remain responsible for accuracy, integrity and references, and should transparently disclose AI use. These are publisher accountability and transparency requirements, not a blanket ban on AI-assisted research, analysis, coding or writing. Recheck the exact proceedings-volume disclosure instructions before submission.

Springer Nature generally does not accept generative-AI-created images, subject to limited policy exceptions. For figures, use the publisher's current rules and verify any claimed exception. This figure-specific rule does not prohibit AI assistance for other research and writing tasks.
