# novelty-check

Read related-work and the current claim. Search task, method, context separately with at least three distinct queries and two public sources, including a no-year-cutoff search for classical cellwise methods. Prioritize CDC, unsupervised TabPFN, TACTIC, ZEN/FOCUS, TaskBridge, TICDA, ForestED and DDC. Verify each relied-on paper and inspect the nearest methods. Write a bounded closest-work delta (same/different task, information, training, context, metric, cost). Return overlapped/incremental/unresolved/proposed delta with queries and limits. Absence of results in a search is not proof of novelty; do not silently replace IDEA with brainstorming.
