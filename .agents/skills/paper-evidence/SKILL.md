# paper-evidence

Read PAPER_GUIDE, CLAIM_EVIDENCE and current conference policy. Use AI assistance for assigned research writing, including drafting substantial prose, editing and synthesis. Mark manuscript stage as draft until authors review it. Human authors verify all prose and sources and retain responsibility; transparently disclose AI use when the applicable publisher instructions require it. Trace every numerical claim to a run/artifact and every literature claim to a checked primary source. Generate result tables/figures from artifacts; no pending result in a completed-study sentence. Separate row anomaly/cell error/label-error tasks. Preserve negative findings and uncertainty during style edits. Verify formatting, citations and source versions; state compile limits. Do not edit research logs/prereg or imply venue compliance from an internal draft. No submission or external sharing without authorization.
